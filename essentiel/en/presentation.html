The studio in brief

The accommodation

An independent furnished studio of 20 m² for 2 guests, with a private 17 m² terrace.

  • Fully equipped kitchen.
  • Fibre and high-speed wifi, adaptable workspace.
  • Secure private parking and an electric-car charging point.

Location

In Chaumeré, in the municipality of Domagné (35), in Haute-Bretagne, near Rennes and on the Rennes–Vitré road.

Opening and booking

The studio opens in 2027. You book directly with us, with no platform commission.
