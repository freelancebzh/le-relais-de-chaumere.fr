Garden camping in Domagné

A necessary clarification about local regulations.

You thought home camping was allowed? So did we!

In a world where even standard campsite prices exceed €20 a night, we wanted to offer a simple, friendly, authentic, and affordable alternative right in our garden.

However, regulations forbid us from doing so.
Official reply from the local municipality (Vitré Communauté): local urban planning bylaws (PLU) strictly prohibit private garden camping in our area.

In short: local regulations prevent small-scale, eco-friendly, and community-driven initiatives, effectively forcing travelers towards large commercial operators and multinational corporations known for tax avoidance.

We are deeply disappointed, as you might be... But rules are rules, and we have to comply with local laws.

Stay with us

While garden camping isn't allowed, we look forward to welcoming you in our self-catering gîte in 2027!
