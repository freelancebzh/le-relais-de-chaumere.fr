Farm campsite, in our garden

A simple, friendly stay near Domagné, Brittany — available right now.

While our guesthouse is being built, we already welcome campers in our garden — a simple, friendly "farm camping" experience that also lets us get used to hosting travellers. It's a complementary offer to the guesthouse, with all the authenticity (and simplicity!) that implies. We're always happy to welcome touring cyclists passing through.

  • ✓ Simple, no-frills pitches, with water and electricity available on request
  • ✓ Access to our own bathroom (you'll be staying right at our home, in a relaxed, friendly atmosphere)
  • ✓ Booking through the same calendar as the guesthouse

We deliberately limit ourselves to 2 pitches maximum to keep things welcoming. Travelling as a group? Feel free to get in touch.
