Our Accommodation

Discover our unique space, designed for your stay.

Bright Studio

2 Guests • 20 m²

A clean, contemporary space with a 17 m² private terrace and high-end equipment. Ideal for a relaxing stay. For the details of our included and optional services, see our seasonal services & rates.

  • ✓ Fibre AND high-speed wifi for your remote-working sessions or your moments of relaxation.
  • ✓ Private, secure, enclosed parking.
  • ✓ Remote-working area that can be set up to suit your professional needs.
  • ✓ Fully equipped kitchen to cook your own meals or try Breton gastronomy in complete independence.
  • ✓ Electric vehicle charging point, ideal for planning your escapes around Upper Brittany.

Note: the rental is available from 2027. To discover the story of the project and our eco-responsible commitments, visit our About Us page (in French) or follow the progress of the works on our Blog (in French).
