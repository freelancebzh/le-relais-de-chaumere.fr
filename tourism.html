🏰 History & Heritage

Vitré (23 min away)

One of the most beautiful medieval towns in Brittany: an 11th-century fortress, cobbled lanes and half-timbered houses.

Châteaugiron (10 min away)

A flamboyant sentinel of the Marches of Brittany, this former antiques town reveals an imposing medieval castle. Its majestic keep and defensive towers tell centuries of history, in the heart of a charming "Petite Cité de Caractère".

🏛️🎨🍽️ Parc Ar Milin' (Châteaubourg)

Around an authentic 17th-century mill, this historic park combines heritage and monumental sculptures. This setting houses a three-star hotel, a gourmet restaurant and a trendy bistro. The perfect combination for strolling and feasting!

🏰⚔️🛡️ Fougères Castle

Visit the largest medieval fortress in Europe! Proudly overlooking a town of character with cobbled lanes and half-timbered houses, this granite giant and its eleven towers offer a grand dive into the history of the Marches of Brittany.

🏰✍️🌿 The Castle of Madame de Sévigné

Walk in the footsteps of the famous letter writer! Flanked by a magnificent French formal garden designed by Le Nôtre, this 15th-century Gothic manor was Madame de Sévigné's favourite place to write.

🎨 Art & Gastronomy

🍔🏆🍷 Cella (Ossé)

This charming village bistro is all about conviviality and sharing. Crowned among the champions of the French Burger Cup, the chef serves homemade, seasonal and utterly delicious food.

🪗La Java Bleue (8 min away)

An unusual guinguette in Amanlis: a waterside restaurant and flea market, with a timeless vintage atmosphere and friendly country cooking.

🧺The market of La Guerche-de-Bretagne (21 min away)

Since 1121, the Tuesday-morning market in La Guerche-de-Bretagne has been an unmissable institution. Ranked among the largest in Ille-et-Vilaine, it brings together residents of more than 40 villages. Focused on short supply chains, this friendly event showcases local producers and boosts local trade across the whole region. There is a "light" version on Saturday mornings.

🍔🍺 Auprès de ma Blonde (12 min away)

Open in Châteaugiron since 2015, this friendly brasserie serves generous burgers and cold beers in an original setting. The perfect spot for a tasty break.

🎭 Escapes

👟Rennes (35 min away)

The Breton capital within easy reach, with the Parliament of Brittany, the historic centre, café terraces and the lively Rue de la Soif.

🚄🗼 Paris (1h19 from Rennes)

You barely have time to finish your coffee on the high-speed train and there it is: the Eiffel Tower. Paris becomes a distant suburb of Rennes!

✈️🌍 Rennes Saint-Jacques Airport (30 min away)

Fly off to Europe! From London to Marseille, this human-sized airport connects you quickly to many destinations without the stress of big terminals, with Air France, KLM, Lufthansa, easyJet, Volotea, Transavia, Air Corsica and Aer Lingus.

🌳✨ Brocéliande Forest (55 min away)

A land of magic and mystery, this legendary forest is home to Merlin and Vivian. An ideal natural setting for a restorative walk full of stories.

🌊 Trips to the Coast and on the Water

🦪St Malo and Cancale (1h10 away)

Perfect for a day trip: a walk on the ramparts of the corsair city, fresh air on the Sillon beach and oysters tasted at the harbour.

🏰Mont St Michel (1h10 away)

A wonder of the Western world in the heart of the bay: a majestic abbey perched on its rock, a timeless medieval village and the unique spectacle of the highest tides in Europe. It is the second most visited monument in France after the Eiffel Tower.

🏝️⛵ The Gulf of Morbihan

A true inland sea dotted with idyllic islands. With its microclimate, boat trips and oyster tastings, the Gulf is a concentrate of the Breton good life.

🦆 Cantache Lake (27 min away)

An immense lake nestled in the countryside near Vitré. Ideal for a restorative hike, birdwatching or trying water sports.

🛶 Vern Leisure Park (19 min away)

Not far from Chaumeré, this green leisure park comes alive in fine weather. With canoeing, supervised swimming and barbecues, it is the unmissable summer meeting place for families.

🎭 Culture

🍷Le Bacchus (Rennes)

In the heart of Rennes, Le Bacchus is a unique, friendly café-theatre. It combines comedies, stand-up shows and tasty food for a successful cultural evening.

🎤Le Zéphyr (10 min away)

Located in Châteaugiron, the Le Zéphyr hall is a dynamic cultural venue. It offers a varied programme of concerts, plays and captivating shows.

💃🎩 Le Cabaret Moustache (Nouvoitou - 11 min away)

Just a stone's throw from Chaumeré, Cabaret Moustache offers a unique music-hall universe combining feathers, acrobatics, singing and gourmet meals for a memorable sparkling evening... in the middle of the countryside!

🎨 Les 3 CHA (Châteaugiron)

Nestled in the castle chapel, this unique art centre hosts monumental contemporary exhibitions. A magical dialogue between medieval heritage and modern creation.

🖼️ The artothèque of the Quai des arts (Vitré)

A dynamic cultural space dedicated to the visual arts. Discover eclectic exhibitions of prints and contemporary photography in the heart of Vitré's cultural hub.

🛸 Unusual

🔎🏰The Henri Chesnais promenade (8 min away)

A world of miniatures awaits in Domagné. This unique and original park reveals a fascinating miniature world. An unusual visit, ideal for families, to admire remarkably precise reproductions made during the retirement of a local farmer.

🪨✨ La Roche-aux-Fées (16 min away)

Located in Essé, this legendary dolmen, the largest in France, is a magical and mysterious site, perfect for a walk through tales, legends and Celtic history.

🌀 Brocéliande, the Gate of Secrets (55 min away)

Let Brocéliande be told to you through a unique sensory experience. This immersive scenographic cinema stirs your senses and awakens the spirits of the magic forest.

🖋️ The Bois-Cornillé Garden (Val-d'Izé)

Walk in the footsteps of the Marquise de Sévigné. This sumptuous Gothic residence and its French gardens tell some of the finest literary pages of the 17th century.

🌲🕵️‍♂️🔒The Church of Vieux Saint-Sulpice (Saint-Sulpice-des-Landes)

Lost at the edge of a forest, this mysterious medieval church hides 15th-century frescoes that are miraculously preserved. A secret time capsule, to be discovered by reservation or during exceptional openings.
