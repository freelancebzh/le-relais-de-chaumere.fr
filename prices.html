Rates & Services

Transparent pricing and tailor-made services for a comfortable stay in our furnished studio guesthouse.

Our Rates: Your Opinion Counts

We haven't set our prices yet. Rather than impose a figure, we'd like to know what feels fair to you for one night in our studio. The survey is anonymous and takes a minute.

Minimum stay: 1 night, 2 nights in high season.

💼 Are you a work-study student, a trainee, in training or on a business trip? We offer an adapted rate for regular or long stays. To get it, simply get in touch with us.

Included Services

  • ✓ 🛏️ Bed linen and bath towels
  • ✓ 🌱 Eco-responsible welcome products
  • ✓ 🧂 Basic groceries (salt, pepper, oil...)
  • ✓ ☕ Senseo coffee machine with welcome pods and milk
  • ✓ 🌐 Fibre-optic Wi-Fi connection

Optional Extras

  • ⚡ Electric vehicle charging (€10 / stay)
  • 🧹 Full end-of-stay cleaning
  • 🥫 The "Late Arrival" basket (local artisan jars to reheat) (for one person, or for two with a bottle of local beer or fruit juice included). An exclusive service to enjoy our selection of local gastronomy from the moment you arrive.
  • ☕ Full breakfast delivered (fresh, local, artisan products)
  • 🧺 Laundry service on request

🌍 Eco-Responsible and Ethical Commitment

  • ☀️ Photovoltaic solar energy: the accommodation produces its own electricity thanks to solar panels on the roof, reducing the carbon footprint of your stay.
  • 🔥 Solar water heater (under study): we are studying a solar water heater to produce domestic hot water directly from the sun, a natural and efficient solution.
  • 🌧️ Rainwater management: we have storage tanks that collect rainwater, which is then used responsibly to water the garden and for maintenance tasks.
  • 🎓 Supportive and useful housing: this tourist rental is open as a priority to young people in training and work-study programmes, with a fair, adapted rate to guarantee them a quality roof during their sessions on site.
  • 📖 Slow tourism: our 100% local welcome booklet and soft-mobility equipment (🚲 bikes available) let you take the time to wander. Plan your programme on our tourism page or get inspired by our ideas for relaxation and reconnecting (in French).
  • ♻️ Zero-waste goal: eco-designed welcome kit (tote bags for the market, jars for bulk shopping, washable kitchen towels, sorting bins).
  • 🔑 Peaceful arrival: self check-in is possible and made easy, so you can travel without the stress of schedules and transport delays.
  • 🎁 Supportive direct booking: by booking here (no middleman), no commission goes to a large platform. If you wish, you can pass on the equivalent (around €5 to €12 for a €60 night) to the A3PC (Association for the Promotion of the Pays de Chaumeré). To learn more about our philosophy, see our dedicated page and "about us" (both in French). To find out how: support the A3PC when you book direct (in French).
